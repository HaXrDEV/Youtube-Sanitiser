# Privacy Policy for YouTube Sanitiser

YouTube Sanitiser does not collect or transmit any personal data.

The data stored is your filter preferences (which content types to hide, your minimum view count and watched percentage settings, and whether to exempt subscribed channels). It is saved with the browser's built-in sync storage: if you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs it to your other devices through your Google account, and if you are signed in to Firefox with Sync turned on, Firefox syncs it through your Mozilla account; otherwise it stays in your browser. The extension itself never sends it anywhere.

To read view counts in any language, the extension also remembers how YouTube abbreviates numbers in your language (for example, that "mio." means a million). This is kept in your browser's local storage on this computer only, never synced or sent anywhere, and contains nothing about you.

When "Keep subscribed channels" is turned on, for live streams or for low view counts, the extension reads your subscribed channel list from the YouTube sidebar so it can exempt those channels. This list is held in memory only while the page is open and is never stored or transmitted.

When "Hide watched videos" is turned on, the extension reads the watch progress bars YouTube already shows on video thumbnails, to tell how much of each video you have watched. It never stores or transmits them.
